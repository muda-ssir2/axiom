Group Discussion Golden Rules

What Evaluators Love (Do's)
  • ✓ Listen actively when others are speaking and take brief notes.
  • ✓ Acknowledge previous speakers: "Building on what Sneha mentioned..."
  • ✓ Support arguments with verified facts, market data, and tech statistics.
  • ✓ Gently bring quieter participants into the conversation.
What Causes Immediate Elimination (Don'ts)
  • ✗ Shouting or interrupting aggressively to claim speaking time.
  • ✗ Making personal remarks or attacking someone's opinion.
  • ✗ Deviating into irrelevant personal anecdotes.
  • ✗ Remaining completely silent for the entire 15 minutes.
