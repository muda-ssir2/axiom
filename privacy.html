Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At AXIOMPATH ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal and academic data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how candidate data is collected, stored, and protected in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) standards.

1. Information We Collect

We collect registration data (name, email address, phone number), academic background (college name, degree, graduation year, CGPA), technical assessment scores, and resume details entered into our Resume Builder.

2. How We Use Candidate Information

Your information is used to personalize your dashboard, recommend matching placement opportunities, generate diagnostic performance radar reports, and forward applications to recruiting companies when you apply.

3. Data Security & Storage

We store all passwords using industry-standard salted hashing algorithms. We never share or sell candidate credentials to unauthorized third parties.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding your data privacy, please reach out to privacy@axiompath.com.
